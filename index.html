03 — Florida, by the reality

Why Florida roofs wear out sooner

Hurricanes, driving rain, intense UV and year-round humidity punish Florida roofs like nowhere else in the country. That’s why small problems here become expensive ones fast.

Wind & storms

Hurricane-season winds lift shingles, break seals and drive rain under the roof surface — damage that often starts invisibly from the ground.

Sun & heat

Relentless UV and attic heat bake shingles year after year, drying them out, curling edges and shedding the protective granules.

Rain & humidity

Heavy daily rains find every weak point — flashing, valleys, pipe boots — and Florida humidity keeps small leaks wet instead of letting them dry.

06 — Measurement

Measured from the sky, verified on the roof

Every Roof Crews Direct proposal is built on a satellite/aerial measurement report of your actual roof: total area in squares, slopes, ridges, valleys and edges — the same quantities the contractor prices from. That means the number of squares in your proposal isn’t a guess or a rounding-up: it’s measured, documented, and you can see it.

We combine that measurement with an on-roof (or attic, where relevant) inspection of condition — because a satellite can measure a roof, but only an inspection can tell you what shape it’s in. Your proposal brings both together: measured quantities + documented condition.

07 — Florida law

Florida law, in plain English

In Florida, roofing work must be performed by — or under — a licensed roofing contractor. That’s exactly how Roof Crews Direct is structured:

  • We are a marketing and lead-generation company. Roof Crews Direct is not a roofing contractor and does not perform roofing work.
  • Your contract is with the licensed contractor who performs the work — their license, their permits, their warranty obligations.
  • The contractor’s identity is confirmed to you before you sign anything — at the proposal stage, you know exactly who will be on your roof.
  • Our markup is disclosed as part of the price you agree to — it is not a hidden fee added later.

10 — Transparent pricing

How our pricing works — the disclosed markup

Most roofing quotes are one opaque number. Ours is an equation we show you:

Contractor’s real cost  +  our disclosed markup  =  your price

  • The licensed contractor sets their cost for your job — labor, materials, dumpster, permits.
  • We add our markup on top, and the contractor knows it — that’s what makes it clean and legal. It is not a referral fee taken in secret from a price that was padded to hide it.
  • You see the structure in your proposal, and the contractor collects the contract price and pays us our share.
  • Because the markup is disclosed, our incentive is simple: a fair price you’d recommend to your neighbor — our growth comes from the 50 homes around yours.

Warranty — in the contractor’s name, in writing

Your work is covered by the licensed contractor’s warranty: their workmanship warranty on the installation, together with the manufacturer’s warranty on the materials installed — both as provided by the contractor and the manufacturer, and both delivered to you in writing at contracting.
